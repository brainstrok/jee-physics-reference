# Complete JEE Physics Formula Coverage Plan

This file is the detailed checklist for all important and minor formulas expected in a complete JEE Physics preparation set.

The aim is to cover not only major formulas but also the smaller but high-frequency equations, assumptions, and special cases that often decide final marks.

## 1) Units and Measurements

### Core formulas
- Speed = distance / time
- Density = mass / volume
- Force = mass × acceleration
- Pressure = force / area
- Work = force × displacement
- Power = work / time
- Dimensional formula of area = [L²]
- Dimensional formula of volume = [L³]
- Dimensional formula of velocity = [LT⁻¹]
- Dimensional formula of acceleration = [LT⁻²]
- Dimensional formula of force = [MLT⁻²]
- Dimensional formula of work/energy = [ML²T⁻²]
- Dimensional formula of power = [ML²T⁻³]
- Dimensional formula of pressure = [ML⁻¹T⁻²]
- Relative error = Δx / x
- Percentage error = (Δx / x) × 100
- Absolute error = measured value − true value

### Measurement concepts
- Significant figures rules
- Least count of instrument
- Random error / systematic error / gross error
- Combination of errors in addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
- Dimensional consistency and dimensional analysis

### Misc important points
- Conversion of units
- SI prefixes
- Checking formulas by dimensions
- Validity of equations using homogeneity

## 2) Vectors

### Core formulas
- Dot product: a·b = ab cos θ
- Cross product: |a×b| = ab sin θ
- Vector addition by triangle law
- Vector addition by parallelogram law
- Resultant magnitude: R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cos θ)
- Direction of resultant: tan α = (B sin θ)/(A + B cos θ)
- Projection of vector: a·b̂
- Unit vector: â = a / |a|
- Vector triple product (conceptual)
- Scalar triple product (conceptual)

### Component form
- a = a_x î + a_y ĵ + a_z k̂
- Magnitude: |a| = √(a_x² + a_y² + a_z²)
- Direction cosines: cos α = a_x / |a|, etc.
- Resolution into components

### Important applications
- Work as dot product
- Torque as r × F
- Area of parallelogram = |a × b|
- Volume of parallelepiped = |a·(b×c)|

## 3) Kinematics

### 1D motion
- v = u + at
- s = ut + 1/2 at²
- v² = u² + 2as
- Average velocity = displacement / time
- Average speed = total distance / total time
- Acceleration = Δv / Δt
- Displacement from area under v–t graph

### Projectile motion
- Horizontal range: R = u² sin 2θ / g
- Time of flight: T = 2u sin θ / g
- Maximum height: H = u² sin²θ / 2g
- Equation of trajectory: y = x tan θ − (g x²)/(2u² cos²θ)
- Horizontal component: u_x = u cos θ
- Vertical component: u_y = u sin θ
- Velocity at any time: v = √(u² + 2g? no, use components)
- Position components: x = u cos θ t; y = u sin θ t − 1/2 gt²

### Relative motion
- v_AB = v_A − v_B
- a_AB = a_A − a_B
- Relative motion in 1D and 2D

### Graphs
- Slope of x–t = velocity
- Slope of v–t = acceleration
- Area under v–t = displacement
- Area under a–t = change in velocity

## 4) Laws of Motion

### Newton's laws
- F = ma
- Weight = mg
- Normal reaction N
- Tension T
- Spring force F = −kx

### Friction
- Static friction: f_s ≤ μ_s N
- Kinetic friction: f_k = μ_k N
- Limiting friction: f_max = μ_s N
- Friction direction opposes relative motion or tendency of motion

### Connected bodies
- For blocks on horizontal plane
- For blocks on incline
- Pulley systems
- Tension equations for each block

### Pseudo force
- F_pseudo = −m a_frame

### Circular motion
- Angular speed ω = v/r
- Centripetal acceleration a_c = v²/r = rω²
- Centripetal force F_c = mv²/r = mrω²
- Banking angle: tan θ = v²/(rg)
- Conical pendulum: T = 2π√(l cos θ / g)

## 5) Work, Energy and Power

### Work
- W = F·s = Fs cos θ
- Work done by gravity: W = mg h
- Work done by spring: W = 1/2 k(x₁² − x₂²)
- Work done in variable force: W = ∫ F·ds

### Energy
- Kinetic energy: K = 1/2 mv²
- Potential energy: U = mgh
- Spring potential energy: U = 1/2 kx²
- Mechanical energy = K + U
- Conservative force: ∇×F = 0
- Non-conservative force losses due to friction

### Power
- Average power = W / t
- Instantaneous power = F·v
- Efficiency = useful output / input

### Work-energy theorem
- W_net = ΔK

### Important relations
- Kinetic energy with angular motion: 1/2 Iω²
- Potential energy in gravitational field: U = −GMm/r

## 6) Centre of Mass and System of Particles

### COM formulas
- x_cm = Σ m_i x_i / Σ m_i
- y_cm = Σ m_i y_i / Σ m_i
- z_cm = Σ m_i z_i / Σ m_i
- R_cm = (Σ m_i r_i)/(Σ m_i)

### Momentum
- P = M V_cm
- Total momentum conservation in isolated system

### Collision basics
- Linear momentum before = after (isolated system)
- Elastic collision: kinetic energy conserved
- Inelastic collision: kinetic energy not conserved
- Coefficient of restitution: e = relative speed of separation / relative speed of approach

### Special formulas
- For two-body collision in 1D, v₁' = ((m₁−m₂)/(m₁+m₂)) v₁ + ((2m₂)/(m₁+m₂)) v₂
- v₂' = ((2m₁)/(m₁+m₂)) v₁ + ((m₂−m₁)/(m₁+m₂)) v₂

## 7) Rotational Motion

### Angular quantities
- θ = s/r
- ω = dθ/dt
- α = dω/dt
- v = rω
- a_t = rα
- a_r = v²/r = rω²

### Equations of rotational motion
- ω = ω₀ + αt
- θ = ω₀ t + 1/2 α t²
- ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ

### Torque and moment of inertia
- τ = r × F
- τ = Iα
- I = Σ m_i r_i²
- Parallel axis theorem: I = I_cm + Md²
- Perpendicular axis theorem: I_z = I_x + I_y

### Energy and momentum
- Rotational KE = 1/2 Iω²
- Angular momentum L = Iω
- τ = dL/dt
- Conservation of angular momentum

### Rolling motion
- Pure rolling condition: v_cm = Rω
- Translational KE + rotational KE
- Rolling without slipping energy equations

### Common MOI values
- Ring: MR²
- Disc: 1/2 MR²
- Solid sphere: 2/5 MR²
- Hollow sphere: 2/3 MR²
- Rod about center: 1/12 ML²
- Rod about end: 1/3 ML²

## 8) Gravitation

### Force and field
- F = Gm₁m₂ / r²
- Gravitational field: g = GM / r²
- Gravitational potential: V = −GM/r
- Potential energy: U = −GMm/r

### Circular orbits
- Orbital speed: v = √(GM/r)
- Orbital time period: T = 2π√(r³/GM)
- Escape velocity: v_e = √(2GM/R)
- Energy of orbiting satellite: E = −GMm/(2r)

### Variation of g
- At height h: g_h = g (R/(R+h))²
- At depth d: g_d = g(1 − d/R)
- At latitude λ: g' = g − Rω² cos²λ

## 9) Properties of Matter

### Stress and strain
- Stress = F/A
- Strain = ΔL / L
- Young's modulus Y = stress / strain
- Bulk modulus K = −ΔP / (ΔV/V)
- Shear modulus η = shear stress / shear strain

### Elastic energy
- U = 1/2 × stress × strain × volume
- Energy density = 1/2 × stress × strain

### Fluid properties
- Pressure P = F/A
- Pressure at depth h: P = P₀ + ρgh
- Buoyant force: F_b = ρVg
- Pascal's law
- Surface tension force = T × length
- Capillary rise: h = 2T cosθ / (ρgr)

## 10) Fluid Mechanics

### Pressure and density
- Pressure: P = F/A
- Hydrostatic pressure: P = P₀ + ρgh
- Specific gravity / relative density

### Buoyancy
- Buoyant force: F_b = ρ_fluid V_displaced g
- Apparent weight = actual weight − buoyant force

### Continuity equation
- A₁v₁ = A₂v₂

### Bernoulli equation
- P + ρgh + 1/2 ρv² = constant

### Viscosity and flow
- Poiseuille’s law (conceptual)
- Stokes’ law: F = 6πηrv
- Terminal velocity: v_t = 2r²(ρ−σ)g / 9η

### Surface tension
- Surface energy = T × area
- Excess pressure in drop/bubble: ΔP = 2T/r or 4T/r

## 11) Thermodynamics

### Heat and temperature
- Q = m c ΔT
- Q = mL
- Linear expansion: ΔL = αLΔT
- Area expansion: ΔA = βAΔT
- Volume expansion: ΔV = γVΔT
- β = 2α, γ = 3α

### Gas laws
- Boyle's law: PV = constant
- Charles's law: V/T = constant
- Gay-Lussac's law: P/T = constant
- Combined gas law: PV/T = constant
- Ideal gas equation: PV = nRT

### Work in thermodynamics
- W = ∫ P dV
- Isothermal work: W = nRT ln(V₂/V₁)
- Adiabatic relation: PV^γ = constant
- Isobaric work: W = P(V₂−V₁)

### First law
- ΔQ = ΔU + W
- For cycle: ΔU = 0, so Q = W

### Specific heats
- C_v and C_p
- C_p − C_v = R
- γ = C_p / C_v

### Carnot efficiency
- η = 1 − T_c/T_h

### Kinetic theory relations
- P = 1/3 ρ c²
- Average kinetic energy per molecule = 3/2 kT

## 12) Kinetic Theory of Gases

### Basic equations
- PV = nRT
- P = 1/3 m N c² / V
- r.m.s speed: v_rms = √(3RT/M)
- Average speed: v_avg = √(8RT/πM)
- Most probable speed: v_mp = √(2RT/M)
- Mean free path: λ = 1/(√2 π d² N/V)

### Degrees of freedom
- Monoatomic gas: f = 3
- Diatomic gas: f = 5 at room temp, 7 at high temp

### Internal energy
- U = f/2 nRT

## 13) Oscillations and SHM

### SHM formulas
- x = A sin(ωt + φ)
- v = Aω cos(ωt + φ)
- a = −ω² x
- ω = 2π/T = 2πf
- T = 2π√(m/k)
- T = 2π√(l/g)
- For spring: ω = √(k/m)
- For simple pendulum: T = 2π√(l/g)
- Energy in SHM: E = 1/2 kA²

### Compound pendulum
- T = 2π√(I/(mgd))

### Damped/forced oscillations (conceptual)
- Resonance conditions

## 14) Waves

### Wave relation
- v = fλ
- k = 2π/λ
- ω = 2πf

### Progressing wave
- y = A sin(kx − ωt)
- y = A cos(kx − ωt)
- Speed = ω/k

### Standing waves
- For fixed endpoints: λ_n = 2L/n
- f_n = n v / 2L
- Open pipe: f_n = n v / 2L
- Closed pipe: f_n = (2n−1) v / 4L

### Beats
- Beat frequency = |f₁ − f₂|

### Doppler effect
- f' = f (v ± v₀)/(v ∓ v_s)
- Sign conventions matter

## 15) Electrostatics

### Coulomb law
- F = k q₁q₂ / r²
- k = 1/(4πϵ₀)
- Electric field: E = F/q
- Field due to point charge: E = kQ/r²
- Force on point charge in field: F = qE

### Potential and energy
- Electric potential: V = kQ/r
- Potential difference: ΔV = W/q
- Potential energy: U = k q₁ q₂ / r
- Energy of dipole: U = −pE cos θ

### Dipole formulas
- Electric dipole moment: p = q(2l)
- Field on axial line: E = 2kp/r³
- Field on equatorial line: E = kp/r³
- Torque: τ = pE sin θ

### Gauss law
- Φ = ∮ E·dA = Q_enclosed / ε₀
- Electric field of infinite line: E = λ/(2πϵ₀r)
- Field of infinite sheet: E = σ/(2ϵ₀)
- Field inside conducting sphere: E = 0

### Capacitance
- C = Q/V
- Capacitance of parallel plate: C = ε₀A/d
- Energy stored: U = 1/2 CV² = Q²/(2C)
- Capacitors in series: 1/C_eq = Σ 1/C_i
- Capacitors in parallel: C_eq = Σ C_i
- Dielectric constant: C = KC₀

## 16) Current Electricity

### Ohm's law
- V = IR
- resistivity ρ = RA/L
- conductivity σ = 1/ρ

### Current and drift velocity
- I = nqAv_d
- J = σE
- v_d = eEτ/m

### Resistance combinations
- Series: R_eq = Σ R_i
- Parallel: 1/R_eq = Σ 1/R_i
- Temperature dependence: R = R₀(1 + αΔT)

### Power and energy
- P = VI = I²R = V²/R
- Electrical energy = VIt
- Joule heating: H = I²Rt

### Cells and circuits
- EMF E = V + Ir
- Terminal voltage: V = E − Ir
- Mixed grouping of cells
- Kirchhoff loop rule: ΣV = 0
- Kirchhoff junction rule: ΣI = 0

### Meter bridge / potentiometer / Wheatstone
- Potentiometer principle
- Wheatstone balance condition: P/Q = R/S

## 17) Magnetism

### Force on charged particle
- F = q(v × B)
- Magnetic force magnitude: F = qvB sin θ
- Radius of circular path: r = mv/(qB)
- Time period: T = 2πm/(qB)
- Frequency: f = qB/(2πm)

### Force on current element
- F = I (l × B)
- Magnetic force on straight wire: F = B I l sin θ

### Magnetic field due to current
- Biot-Savart law: dB = μ₀/(4π) (I dl × r̂)/r²
- Field due to long wire: B = μ₀I/(2πr)
- Field at center of circular loop: B = μ₀I/(2R)
- Solenoid field: B = μ₀ n I
- Toroid field: B = μ₀NI/(2πr)

### Magnetic dipole
- Magnetic moment μ = NIA
- Torque: τ = μ × B
- Potential energy: U = −μ·B

### Motion in magnetic field
- Helical motion
- Cyclotron frequency
- Lorentz force: F = q(E + v×B)

## 18) Electromagnetic Induction

### Faraday and Lenz law
- EMF: ε = −dΦ_B/dt
- Magnetic flux: Φ = B·A = BA cos θ
- Motional emf: ε = B l v
- Self inductance: ε = −L dI/dt
- Mutual inductance: ε₂ = −M dI₁/dt

### Energy in inductors
- U = 1/2 LI²

### RL circuits
- Growth and decay of current in LR circuit
- Time constant τ = L/R

### LC oscillations
- ω = 1/√(LC)
- Energy transfer between electric and magnetic fields

## 19) Alternating Current

### RMS and average values
- I_rms = I₀/√2
- V_rms = V₀/√2
- I_avg for full cycle = 0

### AC elements
- Capacitive reactance: X_C = 1/(ωC)
- Inductive reactance: X_L = ωL
- Impedance: Z = √(R² + (X_L − X_C)²)
- Current in series RLC: I = V/Z

### Power and resonance
- Power factor: cos φ = R/Z
- Average power: P_avg = V_rms I_rms cos φ
- Resonance: X_L = X_C
- Resonant frequency: ω₀ = 1/√(LC)

## 20) Electromagnetic Waves

### Wave equations
- c = 1/√(μ₀ ε₀)
- E = c B
- Speed in medium: v = 1/√(μϵ)

### Energy density
- u_E = 1/2 ε₀E²
- u_B = B²/(2μ₀)
- Poynting vector: S = (1/μ₀) E × B

## 21) Ray Optics

### Reflection
- Angle of incidence = angle of reflection
- Mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
- Magnification: m = −v/u
- For spherical mirrors, f = R/2

### Refraction
- Snell's law: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r
- Refractive index: n = c/v
- Relative refractive index = n₂₁ = n₂/n₁
- Apparent depth: d_app = d / n

### Lens formula
- 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
- Lens maker's formula: 1/f = (n_rel − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂)
- Magnification: m = v/u

### Prism
- Deviation: δ = (n−1)A (for small angles)
- Angular dispersion
- Refractive index from prism formula

### Optical instruments
- Simple microscope magnification: m = 1 + D/f
- Compound microscope: m = (L/f₀)(D/fₑ)
- Telescope magnification: m = f₀/fₑ

## 22) Wave Optics

### Interference
- Path difference Δ = nλ
- Constructive interference: Δ = mλ
- Destructive: Δ = (m + 1/2) λ
- Fringe width: β = λD/d

### Diffraction
- Single slit minima: a sin θ = nλ
- Resolving power: R = λ/Δλ

### Polarization
- Malus law: I = I₀ cos²θ
- Brewster angle: tan i_B = n₂/n₁

## 23) Modern Physics

### Photoelectric effect
- Photon energy: E = hν = hc/λ
- Threshold frequency: ν₀
- Photoelectric equation: K_max = hν − φ
- Work function φ = hν₀

### De Broglie wavelength
- λ = h/p = h/(mv)

### Bohr model
- mvr = nh/2π
- E_n = −13.6 Z²/n² eV
- Radius: r_n = 0.529 n²/Z Å
- Velocity: v_n = 2.2 × 10⁶ Z/n m/s

### Nuclear physics
- Mass defect Δm
- Binding energy = Δm c²
- Radioactive decay law: N = N₀ e^(−λt)
- Half-life: T₁/₂ = ln2 / λ
- Average life: τ = 1/λ

### X-rays
- Bragg law: 2d sin θ = nλ
